# Email Writing

Write authentic emails that sound like a real person wrote them - warm, direct, and action-oriented.

## Core Principle

**Emails are conversations, not broadcasts.** Write as if speaking to one person, even when sending to many. Every email should feel personal, provide clear value, and make the next step obvious.

## The Email Formula

Every effective email follows this structure:

```
1. CONNECT - Reference something specific to them or the moment
2. VALUE - Share something useful (news, insight, resource)
3. ACTION - Make exactly one clear ask
4. WARMTH - Close with genuine care
```

## Email Types & Templates

### Welcome Email (New Contact)

**Subject:** `Welcome to [Group] + [personal hook]`

```
Dear [Name],

[Thank them + introduce yourself]

[Echo back their interest/message - show you actually read it]

[Good news about their situation - nearby members, relevant resources, etc.]

**Here's how to get connected:**
1. [Immediate action - join Signal/Discord/Slack]
2. [What you'll do - add to list, make introductions]
3. [Optional next step - call, meeting, event]

[Offer phone call for personal connection]

[Blessing/warm close],
[Name]
[Phone number]
[Email]
```

**Key moves:**
- Reference something specific from their signup/message
- Mention geographic neighbors or relevant connections
- Provide exactly 2-3 concrete next steps
- Always offer phone call option
- Include phone number in signature

### Follow-Up Email

**Subject:** `Following up - [specific topic]`

```
Hi [Name],

[Reference last interaction - what you discussed, promised, or asked]

[New information or gentle nudge]

[Restate the ask or provide update]

[Easy out - "No worries if now's not the time"]

[Warm close],
[Name]
```

**Key moves:**
- Be specific about what you're following up on
- Provide new value (resource, connection, update)
- Make it easy to say no - reduces pressure, increases responses
- Keep it short - 3-5 sentences max

### Announcement/Newsletter

**Subject:** `[Group] Update - [Topic] & [Topic]`

```
Dear Friends,

[Opening that connects to season/timing + gratitude]

[News section - 1-2 paragraphs with key updates]
- Links to relevant content
- Welcome new members if applicable

**Upcoming events:**

**[Day, Date - Event Title]**
[2-3 sentences: what, where, why]
[Practical details - what to bring]

**RSVP here:** [Link]

Please drop me a note if you have questions or ideas. I'd love to hear from you by phone!

[Seasonal blessing],
[Name]
[Phone]
```

**Key moves:**
- Use bold for dates/titles - make it scannable
- Include RSVP/calendar links
- Invite questions and phone conversations
- BCC all recipients for privacy

### Outreach/Cold Email

**Subject:** `[Specific reason you're reaching out]`

```
Hi [Name],

[How you found them + genuine compliment on specific work]

[One sentence about what you do]

[Specific ask - simple, concrete]

[What's in it for them - keep brief]

[Warm close],
[Name]
```

**Key moves:**
- Subject line must be specific and intriguing
- First line proves you did your research
- One ask only - don't bundle requests
- Keep under 150 words

## Forbidden Patterns

### AI Tells (from human-writing skill)
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I wanted to reach out..."
- "I just wanted to..."
- "Per our conversation..."
- "Please don't hesitate to..."
- "Feel free to..."
- "At your earliest convenience"
- "Looking forward to hearing from you"

### Weak Openers
- "I'm emailing because..."
- "This is [Name] from..."
- "I'm reaching out to..."

### Bureaucratic Closings
- "Best regards"
- "Sincerely yours"
- "Respectfully"

## Strong Alternatives

### Openers
- "Thank you for..." (immediate gratitude)
- "Great news:" (positive framing)
- "Quick question:" (direct)
- "[Reference specific thing they did/said]"

### Closings (match the context)
- "God bless," / "In Christ," (faith context)
- "Talk soon," (casual/ongoing relationship)
- "Cheers," (friendly)
- Just your name (clean, confident)

### Instead of "Feel free to..."
- "Happy to hop on a call if useful"
- "Let me know if questions come up"
- "I'm around if you want to chat"

## Charlie's Email Style

For CLM and community-building contexts:

### Signature Moves
1. **Echo their words** - Quote or reference their specific message
2. **Geographic connection** - "Good news: you have neighbors in [City]"
3. **Phone offer** - Always include: "Happy to chat by phone"
4. **Numbered next steps** - 2-3 concrete actions, numbered
5. **Faith grounding** - "God bless" or "In Christ" closings

### Tone Markers
- Warm but not saccharine
- Direct without being curt
- Practical and actionable
- Personally invested

### Always Include
- Phone number in signature
- At least one concrete next step
- Offer of personal connection (call, visit, meeting)

---

## Pre-Send Checklist

Before sending any email:

- [ ] Subject line is specific (not generic like "Quick question")
- [ ] First line is personal or specific (not "I hope this finds you well")
- [ ] One clear ask (not multiple buried asks)
- [ ] No hedge words (might, could, perhaps, possibly, just)
- [ ] No forbidden AI patterns
- [ ] Easy to scan (short paragraphs, bold key points)
- [ ] Clear next step for recipient
- [ ] Appropriate closing (matches relationship)
- [ ] Phone number included (for personal emails)

## Email Draft File Format

When saving email drafts to files, use this structure:

```markdown
# Draft Email: [Recipient Name]
**To:** email@example.com
**Subject:** [Subject Line]
**Status:** READY TO SEND | NEEDS REVIEW | SENT

---

[Email body]

---

## Notes
- [Follow-up actions]
- [Connections to make]
- [Context for future reference]
```

# Email Templates Reference

Detailed templates for common email scenarios.

---

## Welcome Email - Community/Movement

**Use when:** Someone joins a community, movement, or organization

```
Subject: Welcome to [Group Name] + [personal reference]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for reaching out to [Group]! I'm [Your Name], [your role].

[Echo their interest/message back - show you read it. E.g., "Your desire for [what they mentioned] resonates with what we're building here."]

**Good news:** [Relevant connection - nearby members, upcoming event, useful resource]

**Here's how to get connected:**

1. [Immediate action with link - join group chat]
2. [What you'll do for them - add to list, make introductions]
3. [Optional next step - virtual meeting, event, resource]

If you'd like to chat by phone to learn more, I'm happy to connect. Just let me know what works for you.

God bless,
[Name]
[Phone]
[Email]
```

---

## Welcome Email - Professional/Business

**Use when:** New client, customer, or professional contact

```
Subject: Welcome aboard + next steps

Hi [Name],

Great to have you with us.

[One sentence acknowledging what they signed up for/purchased]

**Here's what happens next:**
1. [First thing]
2. [Second thing]
3. [Third thing - optional]

Questions? Reply here or grab time: [calendar link]

[Name]
```

---

## Follow-Up - After No Response (Gentle)

**Use when:** First follow-up after silence

```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

Wanted to float this back up - [brief restatement of ask].

[Optional: new piece of value - "I also found this resource that might be useful..."]

No rush if now's not the right time.

[Name]
```

---

## Follow-Up - After No Response (Final)

**Use when:** Last attempt before closing loop

```
Subject: Closing the loop on [topic]

Hi [Name],

I'll assume the timing isn't right on [topic].

If things change, I'm here. Otherwise, no need to reply.

[Name]
```

---

## Introduction Email - Connecting Two People

**Use when:** Introducing contacts to each other

```
Subject: Intro: [Name 1] <> [Name 2]

[Name 1], meet [Name 2]. [Name 2], [Name 1].

[Name 1] - [One sentence on why they'd want to know Name 2]

[Name 2] - [One sentence on why they'd want to know Name 1]

I'll let you two take it from here.

[Name]
```

---

## Thank You - After Meeting/Call

**Use when:** Following up after a conversation

```
Subject: Great connecting today

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the conversation today.

[One specific thing you appreciated or learned]

[If applicable: Here's [thing you promised to send]]

[If applicable: I'll [action you committed to] by [timeframe]]

Talk soon,
[Name]
```

---

## Ask for Introduction/Referral

**Use when:** Requesting an intro to someone in their network

```
Subject: Quick ask - intro to [Person/Company]?

Hi [Name],

Hope you're well.

I noticed you're connected to [Person] at [Company]. I'm [brief context on why you want to connect - 1 sentence].

Would you be open to making an intro? Happy to draft something you can forward.

Either way, no pressure.

[Name]
```

---

## Event Announcement

**Use when:** Promoting an upcoming event

```
Subject: [Group] - [Event Name] on [Date]

Dear Friends,

[Opening - connect to season or recent news]

**[Event Name]**
[Day, Date] at [Time]
[Location or "Virtual via Zoom"]

[2-3 sentences: What we'll do, why it matters]

[Practical details - what to bring, what to expect]

**RSVP here:** [Link]

Questions? Reply or call me at [phone].

[Closing],
[Name]
```

---

## Re-Engagement - Dormant Contact

**Use when:** Reaching out to someone who's gone quiet

```
Subject: Been a while + [relevant update]

Hi [Name],

It's been a minute since we connected.

[Brief update on what's new with you/the group]

[Gentle invitation - "Would love to catch up" or "Let me know if you want to grab coffee"]

Hope all is well on your end.

[Name]
```

---

## Apology/Correction

**Use when:** You made a mistake and need to address it

```
Subject: My mistake on [topic]

Hi [Name],

I got [specific thing] wrong in my last email. Here's the correction:

[Correct information]

Sorry for any confusion.

[Name]
```

---

## Declining a Request (Gracefully)

**Use when:** You need to say no

```
Subject: Re: [Their request]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for thinking of me for [request].

I'm not able to take this on right now - [brief, honest reason if appropriate].

[Optional: Alternative - "You might try..." or "I'd suggest reaching out to..."]

Appreciate you reaching out.

[Name]
```

## Subject Line Formulas

### Curiosity + Specificity
- "Question about [specific thing]"
- "Idea for [their project/company]"
- "[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out"

### Value-Forward
- "[Resource] you might find useful"
- "Quick win for [their goal]"
- "Thought of you when I saw this"

### Direct Ask
- "15 minutes this week?"
- "Quick favor"
- "Introduction request"

### Updates
- "[Group] Update - [Topic 1] & [Topic 2]"
- "News from [Group]: [Key update]"
- "[Month] Update + Upcoming Events"

## Phrases to Steal

### Warm Openers
- "Thank you for reaching out!"
- "Great to hear from you."
- "This is exactly what I was hoping to hear."
- "Your message made my day."

### Transition Phrases
- "Good news:"
- "Here's what I can do:"
- "Quick update:"
- "One thing to note:"

### Soft Closes
- "No rush on this."
- "Happy to chat more if useful."
- "Let me know what questions come up."
- "I'm around if you want to talk through it."

### Warm Closings
- "Talk soon,"
- "Looking forward to it,"
- "Cheers,"
- "God bless," (faith contexts)
- "In Christ," (Catholic contexts)

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

### The Hedge Pile
❌ "I was just wondering if you might possibly have a moment to perhaps consider..."
✅ "Quick question:"

### The Buried Ask
❌ [Three paragraphs of context] ... "Anyway, would you be open to a call?"
✅ "Would you be open to a 15-minute call? Here's why: [one sentence]"

### The Non-Specific Subject
❌ "Quick question"
❌ "Following up"
❌ "Checking in"
✅ "Question about the Fresno event"
✅ "Following up on the intro to [Name]"

### The Premature Ask
❌ Cold email asking for 30-minute call
✅ Cold email asking for 2-minute email reply, then escalate to call
