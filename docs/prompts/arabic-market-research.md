# Arabic Market Research

Goal: find angles that are **proven in demand** but **underserved in Arabic**. The general competitor method is in `youtube` (competitor sub-skill) and `yt-viral`; this skill adds the Arabic-market lens.

## 1. Clarify (one message)

- Niche, target countries (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the Gulf, the Levant, North Africa, or all Arabic speakers), and dialect.
- The creator's size and strengths (experience, access, local knowledge).
- What they've already posted (to avoid repeating it).

## 2. Gather evidence

If you have browsing or search tools, use them; otherwise work from what the user pastes and **say clearly what is assumed**.

Look at:
1. **Arabic competitors:** 5–10 channels/accounts in the niche. For each, note top videos by views relative to their usual views (outliers > 3×), formats, upload frequency, dialect.
2. **The English gap:** search the same topic in English. Topics with strong English demand and weak or outdated Arabic coverage are the best opportunities.
3. **Audience questions:** comments on Arabic videos, Arabic Reddit-like communities, Facebook groups, X/Twitter, Quora-style sites, Google's "People also ask" in Arabic. Copy the **exact wording** people use; that becomes titles and hooks.
4. **Search wording:** how people actually type it (dialect vs. فصحى, spelling variants, Arabizi like «mo5", "ezay"), which feeds `arabic-youtube-seo`.

## 3. Seasonal calendar (plan content ahead)

| Moment | Timing | Content angle |
|---|---|---|
| Ramadan | moves ~11 days earlier each year | routines, productivity while fasting, recipes, family, series reactions; viewing shifts to after iftar and late night |
| Eid al-Fitr / Eid al-Adha | after Ramadan / Dhul-Hijjah | gifts, travel, outfits, money, greetings content |
| Back to school | Aug–Sep (varies by country) | study tools, routines, student budgets |
| National days | e.g. Saudi National Day (23 Sep), UAE (2 Dec), Egypt's holidays | local pride, history, local brands |
| White Friday / Black Friday, 11.11 | Nov | deals, tech buying guides |
| Summer | Jun–Aug | travel, side projects, freelancing |

Always check the exact dates for the current year; Islamic dates shift.

## 4. Output

**A. Market snapshot:** 5 bullets: what's saturated, what's growing, the dominant formats, the dominant dialects, what the audience complains is missing.

**B. Competitor table**

| Channel | Size | Outlier video | Why it worked | Weakness to exploit |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**C. 8 angles**

```
Angle: …
Evidence: (outlier / English gap / repeated question, with source)
Why this creator: …
Working title (Arabic): «…»
Hook seed: «…»
Best format: long / short / both
Season fit: evergreen / Ramadan / …
```

**D. First 3 videos to make**, in order, and why.

## Rules

- Separate **evidence** from **assumption**, and label each.
- Never copy competitors' scripts, footage, or thumbnails. Learn the pattern, then make it yours.
- Don't present view counts or trends you didn't actually see.
